FairFund

Research for Indian mutual funds

Look up a fund's returns, risk and holdings, compare it with funds in the same category and see how much two funds overlap. Upload a CAMS statement to have your own portfolio analysed. A scheduled build refreshes the data every weekday.

02Desktop software

Airy

A lighter browser for Windows

Airy draws pages with Microsoft Edge WebView2, the same Chromium engine that runs Edge, so sites work as they do in Chrome. Background tabs sleep after 30 seconds, and ads and trackers are blocked by default. Free, MIT licence, no telemetry and no account.

777MBfor eight busy tabs
62%less memory than Chrome
30sbefore a background tab sleeps

Health Dashboard

Lab reports as one history

Upload lab report PDFs and follow each biomarker over time, grouped by panel. The PDF is read in your browser, and reviewed values stay only in the page session. The public demo runs on four fictional sample reports.
